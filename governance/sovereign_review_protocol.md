- Policy violations > 0.5%

1. **Freeze:** Halt mutation engine.

3. **Audit:** Run compliance suite.

5. **Verify:** Recalculate pricing tiers.
6. **Approve:** Governance review sign-off.
7. **Resume:** Controlled reactivation.
